Privacy-First Guarantee: SoundShare operates 100% on-device. We do not collect, track, store, or sell any personal data or audio recordings.

1 Overview

SoundShare ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed to give you full control over Bluetooth audio sharing and 3D spatial sound. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to zero data collection and transparent device permission usage.

2 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • No Audio Logging or Recording: We never record, save, upload, or transmit your audio streams or microphone feeds.
  • No Telemetry or Tracking: There are zero third-party tracking cookies or ad networks embedded in SoundShare.

3 Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

SoundShare requests only standard Android permissions strictly necessary to operate audio streaming and haptic feedback:

📡 Nearby Devices & Bluetooth (`BLUETOOTH_SCAN`, `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`)

Required to discover, pair with, and stream synchronized audio to multiple Bluetooth headphones and speakers simultaneously.

🎙️ Audio Capture (`RECORD_AUDIO`)

Used strictly in real-time RAM for BeatSync rhythm and frequency transient detection. Audio buffers are processed ephemerally in memory and immediately discarded without being stored or transmitted.

⚡ Foreground Service (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`)

Ensures uninterrupted Bluetooth audio sharing and background spatial audio processing when your screen is locked or while multitasking.

📳 Vibration (`VIBRATE`)

Delivers tactile, audio-synchronized haptic feedback in time with musical beats and bass drops.

🔔 Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)

Displays active playback session controls (Play, Pause, Disconnect) in your system notification shade.
