Privacy Policy for SoundShare

Effective Date: August 31, 2026 • Application ID: com.soundshare.soundshare
🛡️

Zero-Data Collection Guarantee: SoundShare does not collect, record, track, store, or sell any personal information or audio recordings. All operations occur purely on-device in volatile RAM memory.

1 Introduction & Overview

SoundShare ("we", "our", or "the App") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how SoundShare handles data, explaining our architecture of zero data retention and our strict permission requirements.

2 Information We Do NOT Collect

SoundShare is built on a privacy-by-design foundation:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or location data.
  • No Audio Storage or Uploading: Audio analysis data is never saved to device storage or transmitted to any remote servers.
  • No Tracking or Telemetry: There are no advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics, or third-party trackers embedded in the app.

3 Android Permissions & Justifications

To provide multi-device Bluetooth audio sharing and real-time haptic feedback, SoundShare requests the following specific permissions:

Permission Usage & Justification
BLUETOOTH_SCAN
BLUETOOTH_CONNECT		 Required to discover nearby Bluetooth headphones, earbuds, and speakers, and establish low-latency simultaneous audio streams.
RECORD_AUDIO Used exclusively for real-time transient and frequency detection (BeatSync). Audio buffers are processed purely in volatile RAM and immediately discarded. No audio is recorded or stored.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Ensures uninterrupted audio streaming and spatial sound processing when the screen is turned off or when switching apps.
VIBRATE Triggers synchronized tactile haptic vibrations corresponding to musical rhythms and bass notes.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays media playback controls (Play, Pause, Disconnect) in your system notification bar.

4 Third-Party Services & Advertising

SoundShare contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, or data brokers. All digital signal processing (DSP), binaural filters, and spatial audio calculations are executed locally on your device.

5 Children's Privacy

SoundShare does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is fully compliant with COPPA and Google Play Families policy requirements.

6 Developer Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our official Google Play Store app listing page or GitHub repository.
